What LUMEN does

LUMEN is a done-for-you quote follow-up service. It connects to your ServiceM8 account, finds quotes that were sent to customers but never answered, and follows them up by SMS — sent as your business, from a dedicated Australian mobile number, referencing the specific job.

Replies route straight to your phone. When a quote books, the outcome is recorded against the job in your ServiceM8 account, so you can see exactly which jobs LUMEN recovered.

LUMEN works with your ServiceM8 Online Dashboard. There is nothing to install on your phone or iPad — follow-ups run in the background, and replies arrive as normal SMS on the mobile number you nominate.

Connecting your ServiceM8 account

  1. Open the LUMEN listing In your ServiceM8 Online Dashboard, go to Settings, then ServiceM8 Add-ons, and find LUMEN.
  2. Press Connect ServiceM8 shows a permission screen listing exactly what LUMEN can access. Review it and press Allow.
  3. Done You are returned to ServiceM8. There is no separate username or password to create — your ServiceM8 login is all you need.

Connecting does not send any messages. Nothing goes to a customer until your quote list has been reviewed with you and you have approved the go-live. See what happens after you connect.

Permissions LUMEN asks for

LUMEN requests the minimum access needed to find unresolved quotes, message the right person, and record outcomes.

Basic account infoYour business name, so messages are sent as your business and your account is identified correctly.

LUMEN does not access payments, and cannot take payment from your customers. Your data is stored in Australia (Sydney). See our Privacy Policy for full detail on data handling and retention.

What happens after you connect

  1. Quote scan LUMEN reads your job history and builds a list of quotes that were sent but never answered.
  2. Review with you Before anything is sent, we walk the list with you. You choose which quotes are in, which are out, and the mobile number your customers' replies should route to.
  3. Your sending number LUMEN provisions a dedicated Australian mobile number for your business. Every follow-up your customers receive comes from that number, signed off with your business name.
  4. Go live Follow-ups begin on the approved list. New quotes that go stale are picked up automatically from then on.

Replies, bookings and outcomes

Replies

Customer replies route straight through to the mobile number you nominated during setup. You answer as yourself, the way you always have — LUMEN starts the conversation; you close the job.

Bookings

When a chased quote books, the outcome is recorded against the job in your ServiceM8 account. Your job history stays the single source of truth — you can verify every recovered job in your own system, not on a report we produce.

Opt-outs and message rules

  • Every follow-up includes a clear opt-out instruction (Reply STOP).
  • If a customer replies STOP, they are opted out immediately and permanently. LUMEN will never message that number again — for any business using LUMEN, not just yours.
  • Follow-ups are limited to a short, respectful sequence per quote. If there is no response, the sequence ends. Customers are never chased indefinitely.
  • Messages only go to customers you have an existing relationship with — people who asked you for a quote.

Billing

The ServiceM8 add-on is free to connect. The LUMEN service is billed separately by Lumen Pty Ltd:

  • First 90 days free on your existing stale quotes — everything that books is yours.
  • From A$99/month after that. No lock-in contract; cancel any time.

No payment details are ever collected inside ServiceM8. Billing is arranged directly with LUMEN.

Pausing or disconnecting

You are in control at all times.

  • Pause sending: email support@getlumen.com.au and sending stops the same business day.
  • Disconnect entirely: in ServiceM8, go to Settings, then ServiceM8 Add-ons, open LUMEN and switch it off. This revokes LUMEN's access to your account.
  • Data deletion: after disconnecting, you can request deletion of data LUMEN holds by emailing support. See the Privacy Policy for retention detail.

Support

Email support@getlumen.com.au — monitored every business day, and we reply quickly. Include your business name and, if it helps, the job number you're asking about.
